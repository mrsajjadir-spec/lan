Questions

How does qeen know which ad brought a booking?

It reads the chats and comments each ad brings in. When a customer confirms a booking, qeen marks it under that ad and shows you the message as evidence.

Do I stay in control?

Yes. You see and approve every ad, audience and budget before anything goes live.

What do I need to start?

An ad account on Meta, TikTok, Snapchat or Google, and your WhatsApp Business number. qeen walks you through connecting both.

Who pays for the ads?

You do, directly to Meta, TikTok, Snapchat or Google, during the trial and after. Your qeen plan is separate from your ad spend.

What happens after the 7 days?

Pick a plan to keep your campaigns running: Starter at AED 199 a month or Pro at AED 299 a month. Ad spend is always paid directly to the ad platform.
